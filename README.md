# 🛒 E-Commerce SQL Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📌 About This Project

As part of my journey into Data Analytics, I wanted to move beyond practicing individual SQL queries and work on a project that follows the way a Data Analyst approaches a real business problem.

I chose an e-commerce dataset because it provides multiple dimensions to explore — customers, products, orders, sales, revenue, and geography.

I started this project with a simple question:

> **"Before finding insights, do I really understand the data?"**

Instead of jumping directly into analysis, I first explored the database structure, dimensions, dates, customers, products, and key business metrics.

This project helped me strengthen not only my SQL skills, but also my ability to think from a **business and analytical perspective**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to perform exploratory data analysis on an e-commerce dataset using **MySQL** and identify patterns across:

- Customers
- Products
- Sales
- Revenue
- Orders
- Geography
- Product categories
- Customer demographics

The analysis focuses on converting business questions into measurable metrics using SQL.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Structure

The project contains three main tables:

### 👥 Customers

Contains information about customers and their demographics.

Key columns include:

- `customer_key`
- `customer_id`
- `first_name`
- `last_name`
- `gender`
- `birthdate`
- `country`

### 📦 Products

Contains product information and pricing/cost details.

Key columns include:

- `product_key`
- `product_name`
- `category`
- `subcategory`
- `cost`

### 💰 Sales

Contains transaction-level sales information.

Key columns include:

- `order_number`
- `order_date`
- `customer_key`
- `product_key`
- `quantity`
- `price`
- `sales_amount`

# 🔍 Analysis Performed

## 1. Database Exploration

I started by understanding the database structure and available tables.

Questions explored:

- What tables are available?
- How many records does each table contain?
- What columns are available?
- What dimensions exist in the dataset?

---

## 2. Dimension Exploration

I explored the major dimensions of the business.

### Customer dimensions

- Countries represented
- Customer genders
- Customer distribution by country

### Product dimensions

- Product categories
- Product subcategories
- Individual products

This helped establish the overall structure of the dataset before moving into deeper analysis.

---

## 3. Date Exploration

I examined the sales date range to understand the period covered by the dataset.

Analysis included:

- First order date
- Last order date
- Calendar-year span
- Identification of invalid/zero dates

This step also highlighted the importance of handling data-quality issues before calculating time-based metrics.

---

## 4. Customer Analysis

Customer-level analysis included:

- Customer count
- Customer distribution by country
- Customer distribution by gender
- Customer age
- Eldest and youngest customers
- Customers who placed orders
- Revenue generated by individual customers

---

## 5. Product Analysis

Product analysis included:

- Number of products
- Products by category
- Average product cost by category
- Top products by revenue
- Bottom products by revenue

Ranking analysis was used to identify products performing at different levels.

---

## 6. Sales & Revenue Analysis

Key business metrics calculated include:

- Total revenue
- Total quantity sold
- Average selling price
- Total orders
- Total products
- Total customers

I also analyzed revenue across product categories and customers.

# 📊 Key Business Questions

Some of the questions explored through SQL include:

### Customers

- How many customers are there?
- Which countries have the most customers?
- What is the gender distribution?
- How many customers have placed an order?
- Which customers generate the highest revenue?

### Products

- How many products are available?
- Which categories contain the most products?
- Which categories have the highest average cost?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the lowest revenue?

### Sales

- What is the total revenue?
- How many unique orders were placed?
- How many items were sold?
- What is the average selling price?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which countries contribute the most sales quantity?
